# Goals

## G1. Exact step-graphon spectral decomposition

Prove that the linearization at a class-constant equilibrium is the orthogonal
direct sum of a finite quotient operator and cellwise multiplication
operators.

**Status:** proved in ordinary mathematics and reproduced computationally.

## G2. Partition-adapted persistence theorem

Extend the persistence argument of Bramburger--Holzer--Williams to the Banach
space of functions continuous on each cell of a fixed finite partition.
Quotient the global phase-rotation mode.

**Success criteria:**

- existence of a nearby finite-network equilibrium;
- preservation of strict spectral stability;
- explicit hypotheses on cut-norm and cellwise degree convergence; and
- no claim at a degenerate boundary point.

## G3. Application to the six-block family

For every strict interior six-block candidate satisfying the hypotheses, infer
stable equilibria on sufficiently large graph approximants.

**Stronger target:** quantify how the required graph size deteriorates as the
two weak eigenvalues approach zero.

## Non-goals

- The marginal limiting optimizer is not covered by an invertible-linearization
  persistence theorem.
- This repository does not prove a global upper bound over all graphons.
- Exact deterministic finite constructions belong in the direct blow-up
  repository.
